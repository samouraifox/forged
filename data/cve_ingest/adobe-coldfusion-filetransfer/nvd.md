**CVE ID:** CVE-2023-29298

**Description:** An Improper Access Control vulnerability in Adobe ColdFusion allowing "security feature bypass" through unauthorized access to "administration CFM and CFC endpoints" without requiring user interaction.

**Affected Versions:**
- ColdFusion 2018 (all updates through update16)
- ColdFusion 2021 (all updates through update6)
- ColdFusion 2023 (through version 2023.0.0.330468)
